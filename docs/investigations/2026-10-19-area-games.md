## Executive finding and scope

Two games are Cardfight!! Area 4.16 and Buddyfight Area 1.38. They are recovered native GameMaker clients, not web plugins ready for installation. Evidence supports semi-manual two-player card-table simulators with selective convenience automation. Do not promise a complete automatic card-rules engine: card descriptions are stored text, individual abilities sometimes have hard-coded helpers, and many operations are explicit player commands. CF/REPORT.md:82 explicitly states descriptions do not prove ability automation. CF/project/Manual/index.html:92 onward documents player-operated damage flips, soul moves, zone movement, modifiers, locks, markers, and manual card controls. BF controller actions include explicit life deltas, phase selection, taps, zone moves and arbitrary modifiers.

Recommended target: recreate both as modern, server-authoritative manual simulators, using recovered catalog/assets/decks as source material, preserving existing platform rooms, guests/accounts, chat, spectators, rematches and histories. Separate milestones for expanded selective automation. A full automatic rules engine is a different major scope with thousands of card abilities and format-specific rulings, not justified by source recovery alone.

Two-player recommendation is based on paired client state (my/opponent), not a recovered explicit tournament rules specification. No evidence of a complete original server or playable bots was established. Reuse current platform transport rather than emulate legacy network servers.
